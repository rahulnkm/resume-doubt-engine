# Phase 2: the committee map

## Personas

Every hiring committee has these seats. Name the real title behind each for the target role, and drop a seat only when the loop genuinely has no one in it.

| Seat | Who it usually is | What they are judged on |
|---|---|---|
| Gatekeeper | Recruiter, talent partner | Sending candidates who survive the loop |
| Hiring manager | Owns the headcount and the number | Their own metric, and defending the hire |
| Technical evaluator | Engineer or senior practitioner | Whether the work holds up under inspection |
| Partner who will use the work | The team the role serves | Whether their job gets easier |
| Executive or budget owner | Founder, VP, CRO | Return on the salary |
| Peers | Future teammates | Whether the team gets better or worse |

## The chain

One chain per doubt. A persona usually has several.

```
persona
pain            what they are afraid of, in their words
worse           how a bad hire deepens that pain
doubt           the pain turned into a question about this candidate
trigger         what ends the candidacy on the spot
stage           where in the loop it gets tested
passes          what clears the bar
euphoria        what makes them say wow, in their words
proof           the proof of exceptional ability behind that euphoria
doubt keywords  what they screen and red-flag on
proof keywords  the words for the wow, with posting counts where known
obviousness     1 (header raises it) to 4 (only surfaces late)
```

Pain, worse, trigger, passes and euphoria carry a quote and a source label, or are marked as inference. Never present an inference as a quote.

## Obviousness scoring

Score each chain for use by the ordering rule in SKILL.md.

| Score | The doubt is raised by | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The role header alone | unknown company, short tenure, title outruns scope, solo work, seniority |
| 2 | The core function of the job | did they actually do the job, can they hit the number |
| 3 | Craft and depth | can they build it properly, can they deploy and maintain it |
| 4 | Working with other people | will others use it, will they teach, will they hoard |

## Rollup

End the map with every proof ranked by how many personas it wins over. Proofs that win several seats at once are where the strongest bullets come from, and a proof that wins nobody is a bullet not worth writing.

Also split the vocabulary by sub-family when the role has one (for example, two adjacent titles that use different words for the same work). Tailoring later depends on knowing which words belong to which.
